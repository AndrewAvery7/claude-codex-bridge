# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who wrote
it. This is a small tool built to be useful - keep the tone of the project
matter-of-fact and welcoming.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing experience levels, platforms, and toolchains.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for people using the project.
- When you are wrong, say so and move on - that is a strength here, not a
  weakness.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions, and
commit history - and to anyone representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/AndrewAvery7/claude-codex-bridge/security/advisories/new)
(it works fine for conduct reports too and stays confidential) or by contacting
the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

Reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly as circumstances allow.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this policy, and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations.
